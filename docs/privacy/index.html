Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

4K Converter is an iOS app that converts videos from your photo library into widely compatible files — MP4 or MOV video, animated GIF, or M4A audio. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. Your videos never leave your device. The app contains no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

What 4K Converter does with your videos

Videos you select are read from your photo library and processed entirely on your device. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with us or any third party by the app. Converted files are saved back to your photo library or shared only when you explicitly choose to do so, using Apple's standard share sheet. Temporary working files created during conversion are stored in the app's private on-device storage and are cleaned up automatically.

Data collection

4K Converter does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal information
  • Crash reports or analytics
  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • Your videos, photos, or any media content

The app does not use tracking as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, and does not use or share data for cross-app or cross-website tracking. It never presents an App Tracking Transparency prompt.

No advertising

4K Converter shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. Output files carry no watermark, in the free version or with Full Bench.

Photo library access

4K Converter requests photo library access solely to let you pick videos for conversion and to save converted files back to your library. Access is governed by iOS permissions that you can change at any time in Settings. The app works with limited-library access as well as full access.

Subscriptions and payments

Full Bench subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through your App Store account. We never see or store your payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings. Apple's privacy policy applies to the payment process: apple.com/legal/privacy

Children's privacy

4K Converter does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
