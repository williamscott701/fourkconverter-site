What it does

Universal output

H.264/AAC MP4 — the most widely supported video format there is — plus MOV, HEVC, animated GIF and M4A audio extraction.

Keeps your quality

Never upscales, never crops. Resolution up to a 3840 long edge, frame rates up to 60 fps, with High / Standard / Saver quality presets.

Fixes HDR colour

Dolby Vision and HLG footage is tone-mapped to standard BT.709 colour on-device, so nothing looks washed out on other screens.

Splits long videos

Optionally split into 2–8 equal parts, or fixed 30 / 60 / 90-second clips — handy for stories and status updates.

Right way up

Orientation is baked into the pixels, so portrait clips never end up sideways on players that ignore rotation metadata.

Private by design

No accounts, no servers, no uploads. Your videos are processed on your phone and never leave it.
